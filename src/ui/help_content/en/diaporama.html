Slideshow

Start the slideshow from View › Slideshow or with F5. It opens full screen and runs through the photos of the current context.

Starting point

Situation when it startsFirst photo
Viewer openThe photo currently on display
Timeline mode (ribbon)The photo at the centre of the ribbon
Any other viewThe oldest photo in the folder

Ken Burns effect

Every photo gets a gentle zoom and a slow pan:

  • The zoom goes from 0 to 8 % over the whole display time.
  • The direction is random, with a preference for horizontal and diagonal movements (purely vertical ones are toned down).
  • Photos whose aspect ratio differs from the screen are shown with black bars (letterbox / pillarbox) — they are never cropped.
  • While a new photo loads, the animation of the previous one carries on until the next is ready.

Control bar

The bar appears when the mouse moves and hides again after 5 seconds of inactivity.

ControlEffect
◀ PreviousGo to the older photo
Next ▶Go to the newer photo
− / counter / + Shorten / show / lengthen the display interval (1 s to 60 s, in steps of 1 s)
⏸ / ▶Pause / resume the automatic advance
✕Leave the slideshow

Moving manually (◀ / ▶ or the arrow keys) while paused shows the neighbouring photo without restarting the automatic advance — the pause stays on.

Keyboard shortcuts

ShortcutAction

SpacePause / resume
EscLeave the slideshow

For as long as the slideshow runs, the screen saver and the display sleep are switched off: the photos stay visible even if you touch neither keyboard nor mouse. The usual Windows behaviour comes back as soon as you leave the slideshow.

Tip: in timeline mode, scroll the ribbon to the period you are interested in before starting the slideshow — it will begin at the central photo.
