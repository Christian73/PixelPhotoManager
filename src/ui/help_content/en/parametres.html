Settings

Reach the settings through Tools › Settings.

Face recognition — Similarity tolerance

Controls how much two faces must look alike to be placed in the same group during clustering. Range: 25 % to 70 %. Default value: 60 %.

RangeBehaviour
25–30 %Very strict groups — few mixing errors, but the same person under very different angles or lighting can form several groups
31–40 %Balanced groups
41–55 %Wider groups — brings together more variants of the same face
56–70 %Very wide groups — risk of mixing different people

If you change this setting, the groups are recomputed automatically when the dialog is closed. The associations of people already named are not affected.
